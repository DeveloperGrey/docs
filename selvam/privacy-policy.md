# Selvam — Privacy policy

_Last updated 22 September 2026_

The same text is in the app, under Settings → Privacy policy.

## In short

Selvam keeps your money records on your phone. There is no Selvam account, no
server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing is sent anywhere unless
you turn on Google Drive sync.

## What stays on the phone

Entries, accounts, categories, people you owe or who owe you, notes, receipt
photos and settings are kept in the app's private storage. Other apps cannot
read them, and Android's own cloud backup is turned off for Selvam.

## Google Drive sync

Off until you connect your Google account. When on, Selvam copies your data and
receipt photos to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive
(`drive.appdata`), which only Selvam can see. It asks for no other access to
your Drive, and your email address is used only to show which account is
connected. Turning sync off stops it; you can also delete the copy on Drive
from Settings. Google's own privacy policy covers your Google account.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — only when you photograph a receipt.
- **Contacts** — none. When you pick a person, Android hands over only the one
  contact you chose.
- **Fingerprint or face** — only for the app lock, checked by the phone;
  Selvam never sees it.
- **Internet** — only for Google Drive sync.

## Export and import

An export is saved only where you choose. Selvam reads only the file you pick
to import.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Selvam deletes everything on the phone. If you used Drive sync,
delete the backup from Settings first, or remove Selvam from your Google
account's connected apps.

## Children

Selvam is not meant for children and knowingly collects nothing from anyone.
