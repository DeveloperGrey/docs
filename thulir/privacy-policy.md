# Thulir — Privacy policy

_Last updated 26 September 2026_

## In short

Thulir keeps your habits on your phone. There is no Thulir account, no server,
no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app has no internet permission, so it
cannot send anything anywhere.

## What stays on the phone

Your habits, every day you logged them, their schedules and reminders, your
name and your settings are kept in the app's private storage. Other apps cannot
read them, and Android's cloud backup is turned off for Thulir.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — only to show the reminders you set.
- **Alarms & reminders** — so a reminder arrives at the time you chose. Without
  it, reminders still come, a little late.
- **Run at startup** — only to set your reminders again after the phone
  restarts.
- **Vibration** — for the reminder alert.
- **Internet** — none.

## Export and import

A backup or Excel file is saved or shared only where you choose. Thulir reads
only the file you pick to import. What happens to a file after it leaves the
app is up to the place you send it to.

## Moving to a new phone

Android's phone-to-phone transfer, which goes directly between two phones, may
copy Thulir's data when you set up a new phone. It never passes through a
server.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Thulir deletes everything it kept on the phone. Nothing exists
anywhere else, so there is nothing else to delete.

## Children

Thulir is not directed at children and knowingly collects nothing from anyone.
